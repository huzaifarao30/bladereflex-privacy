Blade Reflex – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy covers the Blade Reflex Android app (package com.huzaifarao.bladereflex), developed by Huzaifa Rao.

What the app stores

The game saves your progress on your own device only: coins, unlocked and equipped skins, your win streak, your chosen difficulty and whether you have seen the controls screen. This data is not sent to the developer or anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

What the app does not do

  • No account or login is required.
  • No personal information (name, email, contacts, location, photos) is collected.
  • The Android app shows no advertising and contains no analytics or tracking of its own.
  • There are no in-app purchases.

Third-party technology

The app is made with the Unity engine. Unity may process limited technical information about the device as described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

The browser version of Blade Reflex on CrazyGames is covered by CrazyGames' own privacy policy, which applies to ads and saves on their website.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
